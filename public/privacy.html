Privacy

Short, honest, and readable — what this site knows and what it does with it.

What we collect

This site only collects what you send through the contact form: your name, an optional phone number, your email, and your message. It goes straight to the shop’s inbox (mugsandmulligans@gmail.com) so a person can answer you.

What we don’t do

No advertising trackers, no analytics cookies, no pop-ups, no mailing-list sign-ups hiding in the footer. The interactive map uses Google’s map embed, which may set its own cookies under Google’s policy once you interact with it.

Your notes

Messages are kept only as long as needed to answer you. To ask for a copy or a delete, email mugsandmulligans@gmail.com with "privacy" in the subject.
